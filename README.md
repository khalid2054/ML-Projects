## 📁 Projects

### 1. Insurance Charges Prediction
- **File:** `insurance_cleaned.csv`
- **Algorithm:** Linear Regression
- **Goal:** Predict medical insurance charges based on age, BMI, smoking status, region

### 2. Titanic Survival Prediction
- **Files:** `titanic.ipynb`, `titanic_eda`, `logiastic.py`
- **Algorithm:** Logistic Regression
- **Goal:** Predict passenger survival on the Titanic
- **Includes:** Full EDA + feature engineering + model pipeline

---

### 3. Video Game Sales EDA
- **Files:** `VSALE.ipynb`, `vgsales.csv`
- **Type:** Exploratory Data Analysis
- **Goal:** Analyze global video game sales by genre, platform, publisher, and region

---

### 4. Stroke Prediction Pipeline
- **Folder:** `stroke project/`
- **Algorithm:** Full ML Pipeline
- **Goal:** Predict stroke risk based on health indicators
